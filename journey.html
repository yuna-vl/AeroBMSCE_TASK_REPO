// 02. COMPETITIVE DIVISIONS

The Four Sub-Teams

Our competitive spirit and engineering specialization shine through four dedicated sub-teams representing BMSCE at premier national and international arenas.

[DIV.01] Heavy Lift

Team Yaksha

International Competitions

Specializes in heavy-lift fixed-wing aircraft design for global collegiate arenas including SAE International Aero Design West (USA). Focuses on advanced aerodynamic simulation, composite wings, and high-efficiency payload delivery under strict dimensional limits.

[DIV.02] Aeromodelling

Team Blue Impulse

Aeromodelling & Design

Pioneers in high-precision aeromodelling and national-level aircraft challenges. Winners of the 1st Place Overall Nationally at SAE-India with ₹1,00,000 cash prize, setting industry benchmarks in design report documentation and CAD modeling.

[DIV.03] Drones

Team Flying Phantoms

Autonomous Drones

Drives autonomous drone architecture, multi-rotor stability, and convertible fixed-wing surveillance platforms. Champions in the SAE Drone Design Challenge and Autonomous Innovation awards for custom sensor fusion and avionics.

[DIV.04] FPV Racing

Team Vectors

Drone Racing & Agility

Focuses on high-speed FPV (First-Person View) drone racing, agility tuning, and dynamic throttle response. Consistently securing podium placements including 1st Place at Global Academy of Technology and 2nd Place at NITK Surathkal.
