// 02. TECHNICAL ROADMAP

Research & Development Initiatives

Propel Lab–3 actively conducts applied research in avionics hardware, aerodynamics, and autonomous defense systems.

[R&D.01] Avionics

Indigenous Flight Controller

End-to-end hardware and firmware design for custom multi-rotor and fixed-wing stabilization, featuring low-latency sensor loops and telemetry redundancy.

[R&D.02] Electronics

Electronic Speed Controller (ESC)

High-efficiency propulsion electronics optimized for extreme thermal dissipation, high burst current, and regenerative braking under flight loads.

[R&D.03] Robotics

GPS-Denied Navigation Systems

Vision- and optical-flow-based autonomous positioning and SLAM navigation allowing precise trajectory tracking without satellite reception.

[R&D.04] Airframe

Surveillance Aircraft Platform

Re-configurable Multi-Mission Fixed-Wing UAV equipped with convertible propulsion mechanisms and an integrated modular payload bay for reconnaissance.

[R&D.05] Restricted

Confidential Defense Project

In strategic collaboration with DRDO (Defence Research and Development Organisation). Specific technical parameters and flight profiles restricted.

[R&D.06] Aerodynamics

Morphing Wingtip Mechanisms

Adaptive aeroelastic mechanisms designed to reduce induced drag, optimize lift distribution, and enhance overall loiter endurance.

[R&D.07] Control

Deployable Micro-Fins Stability

Directional stability enhancement using deployable micro-fins for active yaw control and gust alleviation in turbulent atmospheric conditions.

[R&D.08] Pipeline

Partner Strategic Outcomes

Early access to industry-ready aerospace talent, association with top-ranked teams, and high-impact brand visibility (20,000+ digital, 50,000+ on-ground impressions).
