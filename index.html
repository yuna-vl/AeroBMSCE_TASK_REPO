// 01. INSTITUTIONAL FOUNDATION

Who We Are

AeroBMSCE is the official aerospace, aeromodelling, and unmanned systems innovation club of BMS College of Engineering, operating directly from Propel Lab–3. Established in 2012, the club brings together engineers from over 10 academic departments to design, build, and test advanced aircraft, drones, and autonomous systems.

  • 01

    Competitive Aircraft & UAV Design

    Aerodynamic analysis, carbon-composite layups, and micro-class precision payload systems built to international competition standards.

  • 02

    Drone Racing & Autonomy

    High-agility FPV racing platforms and autonomous fixed-wing surveillance platforms tested under rigorous mission constraints.

  • 03

    Industry-Grade Fabrication & Testing

    Hands-on validation in telemetry simulation, dynamic thrust testing, and structural finite element analysis.

// 03. ACTIVE R&D & AIRFRAME INITIATIVES

What We Build

Previewing indigenous hardware, autonomy suites, and defense research initiatives by AeroBMSCE.

[DEV.01] Avionics

Indigenous Flight Controller & ESCs

Hardware & Firmware

End-to-end hardware and firmware design for custom flight control systems and high-efficiency Electronic Speed Controllers (ESCs) tailored for extreme flight profiles.

[DEV.02] Autonomy

GPS-Denied Navigation Systems

Robotic Vision & SLAM

Computer-vision and multi-sensor fusion algorithms allowing autonomous UAV positioning, obstacle avoidance, and mission execution in contested environments.

[DEV.03] Airframe

Multi-Mission Surveillance UAV

Convertible Propulsion

Re-configurable fixed-wing platform featuring convertible propulsion systems and an integrated modular payload bay for aerial mapping and reconnaissance.
